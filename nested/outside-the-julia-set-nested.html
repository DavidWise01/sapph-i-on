What's outside a Julia set.

Keep doing one thing to a point: square it, add c. Some points stay close forever. That's the inside. The rest run off to infinity. That's the outside. The Julia set is the fence between them. The outside isn't empty space. It has a map, and every point in it has an address.

Small words. Pick a spot. Square it and add a number, again and again. If it stays near home, it's inside. If it zooms away, it's outside. The fence is the Julia set. Every outside spot gets two labels: how high above the fence it is (its ring) and which way it runs off (its ray). It's latitude and longitude, just for escaping.
