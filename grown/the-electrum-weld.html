THE ELECTRUM WELD

gold meets silver; the faster atom wins the crossing

THE RULE

Gold sits on the left and silver on the right, with 1% vacancies. A vacancy swaps with a neighbour when the neighbour jumps: gold with probability pAu, silver with pAg, and the seed makes them unequal.

Tap to stir vacancies in a patch. The same ?seed= always gives the same run.

WHAT IS CLAIMED

LITVacancy-mediated interdiffusion; unequal jump rates are the Kirkendall effect (1947). Every 30 steps the gold, silver and vacancy counts must be conserved, or the page throws.
