small words. neon has 10 tiny electrons, and every seat is full, so it's calm. other atoms want that calm too. nitrogen grabs 3 extra, oxygen grabs 2, sodium throws 1 away, and then they all have exactly neon's 10. the fewer protons in the middle, the weaker the hug, so nitrogen's 10 float widest, oxygen's a bit closer, neon's closer still, sodium's tightest. that's your nesting: you, sodium, in the middle, wrapped in neon, wrapped in oxygen, wrapped in nitrogen. and when you decide something, a nerve opens a door, sodium runs in, and the cell says yes all at once. it can't say half a yes.
