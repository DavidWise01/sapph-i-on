The Diagonal

Four space diagonals each have squared length 108.

← The Dual The Bonds →

LIT Among the 8 corners, four pairs differ in all three coordinates. Each pair spans 6 in x, y, and z, so the squared length is 3×36 = 108.

AMBER Space diagonal is the name of a corner-to-opposite-corner segment.

WALL 108 is a squared grid length, not a measured distance in meters.
