ELI5: before, each of the six points sang its own little note. Now we do something different: we hang ONE tuning fork right in the middle (the blue gem), and the six points become six tiny hammers, each with a string running to the fork. They take turns tapping it — but in a clever woven order. Three of the points are a "top team" (the plus points) and three are a "bottom team" (the minus points), and they alternate: top-tap, bottom-tap, top, bottom, top, bottom — six taps every spin, zippering the two teams together. Because a tuning fork barely loses its hum, tapping it six times fast keeps it singing one steady, pure note — the same "A" a real tuning fork gives (the note orchestras tune to). And here\u2019s the neat bit: if all six taps land perfectly in time, they push the fork six times harder than a single tap — six little nudges stacking into one strong, clear ring. So the whole six-pointed diamond becomes one instrument: six hammers, two teams zippered together, all ringing a single gem in the center. Bong… and it holds.
