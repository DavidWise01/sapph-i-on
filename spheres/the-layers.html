The Layers

Seven layers of 49 cells make 343.

← The Seal The Checker →

LIT Each fixed z from 0 through 6 contains 7×7 = 49 cells. Seven such layers sum to 343, and 7×49 = 343.

AMBER A layer is a slice chosen for looking, not a claim about stacking in space beyond the grid.

WALL Nothing here is a measurement of a physical slab.
