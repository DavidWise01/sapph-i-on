small words. everything we measure is made of how heavy, how long and how many seconds. light's speed says a second is just a very long metre. gravity says a kilogram is just a very, very tiny metre. now everything is metres. the tiniest wiggle of nature says one very small square is exactly 1. then everything folds to a dot, and all that's left is one yes-or-no: does it need a square root or not.
