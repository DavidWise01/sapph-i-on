what a spinor is

Two bound electrons e(e) — the S.S dumbbell. Spin it: the electron's space angle is θ, but its quantum state turns at θ/2 — half speed. The yo-yo.

360°: space is home but the state points the other way → −1, flipped. 720°: state finally home → +1. It takes two turns. That is the spinor.
