The Electrum Elevator

This is the lobby of a tower with 21 floors and two cars, Aurum and Argentum. Tardigrade passengers arrive at random floors (a Poisson process). Each car follows one rule: keep going in its direction while anyone ahead wants it, otherwise turn around (the classic elevator, or SCAN, algorithm). Bunching, waves and lulls emerge from that rule. Every trip a car completes is a reservation, with the car as the id and its trip count as the epoch. Tap a floor to call a car there.

LIT: the SCAN rule (also used for disk heads) never strands a request, so waiting time is bounded. Poisson arrivals give memoryless gaps. INVENTED: the tower and its passengers. ANALOGY: the cars are gold (79 %) and silver (21 %) in the electrum ratio; Aurum serves 79 % of the calls when both run.
