WINNERHolographic counting: the most information a region can hold grows with its boundary area, so a simulator of a region of radius r needs at most ~ A/(4 ℓ_P² ln 2) bits of state. For the observable universe (r ≈ 4.4×10²⁶ m) that is about 10¹²³ bits.
CHALLENGERVoxel counting: one bit per Planck volume, ~(r/ℓ_P)³. It is far larger (about 10¹⁸⁴ for the universe) and overcounts against the area bound.
