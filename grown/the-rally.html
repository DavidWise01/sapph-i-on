The Rally for Σ = 0

Three hundred marchers carry charges in the 0-sphere's proportions: a third are dots (0), a third vectors (−1) and a third edges (+1). There is no choreographer. Each marcher follows boid rules (stay near your neighbours, match their heading, don't collide) and adds one more: charges of opposite sign pull together, and like charges push apart. Neutral columns and blocks form on their own, and the poster records them. Tap to raise a banner, which every marcher heads toward. Each formation the crowd reaches is a reservation on the ledger.

LIT: boids (Reynolds, 1987). Constructivism (Rodchenko, El Lissitzky, 1920s) used red, black, diagonals and photomontage. MODEL: separation, alignment, cohesion, plus a Coulomb-like term with a hard core. ANALOGY: a crowd as a 0-sphere: Σ charge = 0 is checked every frame.
