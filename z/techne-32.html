0-sphere

TECHNE · domain 32 · 2 dots · 2 vectors · 2 edges · Σ = 0

THE RULES

Two dots (q = 0) are held loosely to the centre, two vectors (q = −1) and two edges (q = +1) move freely, and every pair obeys the same rules: opposite charges pull together, like charges push apart, nothing gets closer than its radius, and the motion slowly damps out. Nobody places the parts; the shape they settle into is the one they find. This sphere’s seed comes from its own address, so it always settles the same way. Drag any part to disturb it.

WHAT THE PAGE PROVES

LIT on every frame the net charge 0 + 0 − 1 − 1 + 1 + 1 is recomputed from the parts and must be 0, or the page throws.

AMBER a toy electrostatics, not a physical claim.
