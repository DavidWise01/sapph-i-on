LITA charged particle moving faster than light's phase velocity in a medium (β > 1/n) emits Cherenkov light in a cone with cos θ = 1/(nβ). The Frank–Tamm formula gives a photon count per unit wavelength ∝ 1/λ², so the spectrum is weighted to short wavelengths, hence the blue glow of reactor pools. In water (n ≈ 1.33) the threshold is β = 0.752, an electron kinetic energy of about 0.26 MeV.
