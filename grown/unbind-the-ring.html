Unbind the Ring

Chapter 8: the rising. Kessa comes back down into the moss carrying the truth about the aquifer, and the walls of the warrens take up her mark: a tun breaking an electrum ring. The mark spreads by one rule. A wall section near marked walls catches it with a probability that grows with how many of its neighbours are marked. Frustulate whitewash (patrols) covers marks at a fixed rate, and a covered wall is wary for a while. Waves, refuges and sudden tipping points all emerge from that. Every new district reached is a reservation on the ledger. Tap to spray a mark yourself.

LIT: contagion models with recovery (SIRS) show thresholds and waves. Stencil graffiti is a fast, reproducible protest medium. INVENTED: the rising and its mark. MODEL: SIRS on a grid, with infection 1 − (1 − β)^k for k marked neighbours.

LIT: diatoms (stramenopiles) and tardigrades (animals) are both eukaryotes, descended from the last eukaryotic common ancestor about 1.8 to 2 billion years ago. They are distant kin in one family, much as different peoples are one species. Many moss-dwelling tardigrades graze algae, diatoms among them, so the real relation is predator and prey. INVENTED: their castes, houses, war and politics. Story inspired by Red Rising by Pierce Brown. All names, characters and text here are original.
