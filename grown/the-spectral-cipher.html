THE SPECTRAL CIPHER

LITIn 1868 Janssen and Lockyer saw a yellow line at 587.6 nm in the Sun's spectrum that matched no known element; Lockyer named it helium. It was found on Earth only in 1895. Starlight is a message written in element lines; spectroscopy is how it is read. Wavelengths below are standard air values from the NIST Atomic Spectra Database.

MODELType a word that can be spelled with element symbols. The "star" absorbs at the lines of exactly those elements, in noise. The decoder knows only the line lists: it matched-filters the spectrum for each element and the word emerges from the light. Raise the noise until it breaks.
