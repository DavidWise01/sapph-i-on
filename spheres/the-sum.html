The Sum

Each axis sums to 1029, and the mean cell is the center.

← The Twenty-Four The Outside →

LIT The sum of x over all cells equals the sum of y and the sum of z. Each is 49×(0+…+6) = 49×21 = 1029. Dividing by 343 gives 3, the center coordinate.

AMBER Mean cell is a name for that average coordinate.

WALL The average is not a center of mass of a physical object.
