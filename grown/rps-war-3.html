How it ends up (Node/numpy, 1,000,000 rounds × 3 seeds per policy)
It depends only on the winner’s choice. stay: locked from round 1. P1 is Judge forever, and A and B split wins 50/50. winner takes Judge and loser to Judge both rotate every round, giving each player about 1/3 of the time in each seat and about 1/3 of the wins. Throws come out 1/3 each and the mean war is 0.50 ties per round, with war length k at P = (2/3)(1/3)^k (longest 12–13). That matches the RPS Nash equilibrium, but only by construction, because the throws were uniform random.

The winner’s choice is run as 3 separate policies: the winner keeps the seats, the winner swaps into Judge, or the winner sends the loser to Judge. With identical random players, no seat pays, so there is no self-interested reason to prefer one. Seeds are 1–3 (numpy here; the page uses its own generator).
