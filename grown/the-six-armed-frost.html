THE SIX-ARMED FROST

vapour, a seed, and six-fold symmetry from a local rule

THE RULE

A hexagonal grid holds water. Frozen cells and their neighbours are receptive and keep their water, gaining γ; other cells share vapour by diffusion α. A cell freezes once it holds 1 or more.

Tap left to dry the air, right to wet it; the flake regrows. The same ?seed= always gives the same run.
