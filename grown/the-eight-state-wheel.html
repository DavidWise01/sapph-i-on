THE EIGHT-STATE WHEEL

the 8 q.d states eat each other in a circle

THE RULE

Each cell holds one of the 8 q.d states. A cell in state s moves to s+1 (mod 8) when at least θ neighbours are already in s+1. State 7 gives way to state 0, closing the wheel.
