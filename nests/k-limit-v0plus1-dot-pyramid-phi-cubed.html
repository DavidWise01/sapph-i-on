Why four, solved continuously

the ratio the pyramid is trying to reach

Force the edge-to-vertex ratio onto the golden ratio and solve for the base:

2n / (n+1) = φ  →  n★ = φ / (2 − φ) and 2 − φ = 0.3819660113 = 1/φ² — the same number
n★ = φ · φ² = φ³ = 4.2360679775 φ³ = 2φ + 1 = √5 + 2  ·  ⌊φ³⌋ = 4

The golden point is at 4.236 and pyramids only come in whole bases, so the sequence lands on 4 — the nearest integer below the exact answer. Your base of 4 is the floor of φ cubed. That is not a resemblance; it is the solution to the equation.LIT
