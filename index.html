THE RULES

local rules only — nothing below is placed or timed

Every 0-sphere carries 2 dots (0, 0), 2 vectors (−1, −1) and 2 edges (+1, +1). A free vector reaches for the nearest free edge on a neighbour, and when they meet they bond. A bond is −1 joined to +1, so it is 0. Bonds stretch and break when the world runs hot. Bonding cools the world down, and new 0-spheres arrive at a logistic rate. When a cluster grows big enough, a keeper emerges at its heart and draws free 0-spheres in. Every eight bonds in its cluster opens a domain, up to 64.

Tap a keeper to visit it, or tap anywhere else to stir the field. The same seed always grows the same world.

WHAT THE PAGE PROVES

LIT every frame it checks that each 0-sphere sums to 0, that each bond is a −1/+1 pair, and that the world totals 0. If any of these fails, the page throws.

AMBER keeper names follow the order they emerge in and are assigned voice.
