DIAMOND · NV

LITA nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centre is a nitrogen atom beside an empty site in the diamond lattice. Its electron spin can be read with light at room temperature. In CVD diamond with ¹³C depleted to 0.3%, Balasubramanian et al. (Nature Materials 8, 383, 2009) measured a room-temperature spin-echo T₂ of 1.8 ms (other centres in the sample: 0.5–2 ms). Natural diamond is about 1.1% ¹³C. The nuclear spins of ¹³C are what make the electron forget.
