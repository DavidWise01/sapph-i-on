Where you stand

The secret is which half you're on.

Draw heavy, far and long as the three edges of a cube. Its corners split into two interlocking pyramids, gold and violet. The one bit that survives the fold is simply which pyramid a thing lives on.

Turn the cube to look straight down its corner and it becomes a hexagon with you in the middle. Your corner, heavy × far × long, is gold.

In small words: light says a second is a long metre. gravity says a kilogram is a tiny metre. now everything is metres. the tiniest wiggle in nature says one tiny square is exactly 1. then everything is just a number, and only one question is left: does it need a square root?
