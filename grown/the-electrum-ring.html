The Electrum Ring

A ring around a single living cell: 79% gold and 21% silver by mass. Gold is almost twice as dense as silver, so by volume the silver claims more of the ring than its 21% suggests. The ring is drawn by volume: each of the 72 segments is one 72nd of the metal. Drag to turn it; slide to change the mix.
