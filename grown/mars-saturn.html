MARS · SATURN

LITMars is reddish-orange because its dust is rich in ferric iron oxides, which absorb blue and green. Saturn's pale butterscotch yellow comes from ammonia-ice clouds under a photochemical haze. Both reflect sunlight; neither glows on its own.

MODELReflectance curves are simple smooth shapes chosen to mimic the published trend (Mars rising steeply past ~550 nm, Saturn gently past ~450 nm). They are not measured spectra; the critique flags this. The colours come from sunlight (5772 K) times reflectance through CIE, Bradford-adapted. Nesting: Mars an orange core, a purple twilight band, a green aurora oval (Saturn's are real but ultraviolet; this is INVENTED for the palette), Saturn's yellow rings, void.
