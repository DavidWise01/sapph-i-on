WINNERKolmogorov complexity (Kolmogorov 1965; Solomonoff 1964; Chaitin 1966): the length of the shortest program that prints the string. It is uncomputable, but any compressor gives an upper bound, and here LZ78 (Ziv & Lempel 1978) is that bound.
