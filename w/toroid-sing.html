what is not being claimed
This is a finite occupancy picture on a torus of revolution. It is not a derivation of the exclusion principle, not a count of states in a real atom, and not a claim that spacetime is a 3×3×3 lattice. The beauty is the sheet. The math that survives the beauty is the double cover and the 81 = 9×9 tick count.
