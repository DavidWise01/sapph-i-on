JEOVA SANCTUS UNUS

LITNewton signed alchemical papers with the pseudonym Jeova sanctus unus ("Jehovah the holy one"), an anagram of Isaacus Neuutonus (Latin for Isaac Newton, with uu for w, and I/J and U/V interchangeable as in his Latin). A manuscript now in the Huntington Library (Babson MS 1006, edited by the Chymistry of Isaac Newton project, Indiana University) lists over thirty such phrases. A few are tested below, exactly as transcribed there.
