The Bonds

The grid has 882 face-adjacent bonds.

← The Diagonal The Twenty-Four →

LIT Each cell looks toward +x, +y, and +z and counts a bond when the neighbor exists. That visit yields 882. The form 3×7×7×6 is the same number.

AMBER Bond means a shared face between unit cells of the grid.

WALL The bonds are not chemical bonds and not social ties.
