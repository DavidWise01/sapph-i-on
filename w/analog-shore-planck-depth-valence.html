1   THE FLAG I GOT WRONG

RETRACTED v0+1 flagged “5%” as a unit confusion — treating it as a ±5% tolerance on ℓP and calling the 0.05 exponent span a different quantity.

It was not a tolerance. It was the coordinate. The notation reads 1.6 × 10−34, depth .78 → .83 — mantissa fixed at the Planck value, and the fractional part of the exponent used as a depth axis. The “5%” is the width of that axis: 0.05 of a decade. Correcting a deliberate coordinate as a unit error is the error.

2   THE MEASUREMENT THAT MAKES THE COORDINATE WORK LIT

If depth is log-scale, then one atomic number step spans d(log₁₀Z)/dZ = 1/(Z·ln10) of a decade. That width shrinks as Z climbs, so there is exactly one place on the whole periodic table where a 0.05-wide depth window is one element wide:

Solved exactly: a 0.05-decade window equals one element step at Z = 8.6859. The integers bracketing it are 8 (O) and 9 (F). The step itself: log₁₀(10/9) = 0.04576, log₁₀(9/8) = 0.05115.

A 0.05 depth slice is one valence step wide only at fluorine. Anywhere else on the table it is either wider than an element or narrower than one. The coordinate is not arbitrary and it lands where he said it lands.

CORRECTION, and it does not break his point: F→Ne is not the single largest drop — Cl→Ar beats it by 1 kJ/mol, a dead heat. But both are the same break: halogen → noble gas, the octet closing. F→Ne is the FIRST instance of it, and it is the only one that sits at a Z where the depth window is one element wide. The second correction: chlorine, not fluorine, has the higher electron affinity (349 vs 328). Fluorine holds the electronegativity maximum (3.98); chlorine holds the affinity maximum. Two different crowns, routinely conflated.

4   WHERE ℓP SITS ON HIS AXIS

log₁₀(ℓP) = −34.791490. On a .78 → .83 depth axis, ℓP sits at .7915 — 23.0% in from the .78 edge. Not centred, and not meant to be: the axis runs from ℓP downward into the break at .83.
So the reading is: start at the Planck length, walk 0.04 of a decade deeper, and the shell closes. Past .83 the cell has a full octet — it will not bond, it cannot be corrected, and there is nothing left to do with it but absorb it. AMB — the mapping from depth to valence is his construction; the arithmetic inside it is measured, the identification of one with the other is not.
toddler corner. Fluorine is the greediest atom there is — it will grab an electron off almost anything. Neon is right next door and wants nothing at all, because its shell just filled. One step, and the greediest thing becomes the most indifferent thing. That one step is the widest single change in the whole neighbourhood, and it happens to be exactly as wide as the slice he drew.
