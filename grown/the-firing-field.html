THE FIRING FIELD

one spark can stay alone or take the whole grid

THE RULE

Integrate-and-fire neurons on a grid get random drops of charge. A neuron that crosses threshold fires, resets, and gives charge w to its 8 neighbours, which can fire in the same instant.

Tap a neuron to fire it. The same ?seed= always gives the same run.
