THE HUNGER LATTICE

+1 prey, −1 predators, and the waves between them

THE RULE

Prey (+1) breed into empty neighbouring cells. Predators (−1) eat a neighbouring prey to breed, wander into empty cells, and die at a fixed rate. Every rule is local.

Tap to release a pack of predators. The same ?seed= always gives the same run.

WHAT IS CLAIMED

LITA stochastic lattice Lotka–Volterra system. Every 30 steps the tracked populations must equal a fresh count, or the page throws.

AMBERReading prey and predators as +1 and −1 is the site’s language.
