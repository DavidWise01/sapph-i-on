WINNERThe area law: S = A / (4 ℓ_P²) nats, so N = A / (4 ℓ_P² ln 2) bits. That is 1/(4 ln 2) ≈ 0.3607 bits per Planck area (Bekenstein 1973; Hawking 1975; holographic bound, ’t Hooft 1993 and Susskind 1995).
CHALLENGERVolume counting: one bit per Planck cell, so N ∝ (r/ℓ_P)³. This is the naive expectation from local field theory, and it overshoots the area bound for every r ≫ ℓ_P.
