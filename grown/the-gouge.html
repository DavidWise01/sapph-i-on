The Gouge

A block of pear wood with Patricia drawn on it. Thirty gouges cut it at once, and none of them knows the drawing. Each one follows a single rule: cut along the contour of the wood's “pull” toward the figure, and stop where the wood has already been cut. The background lines that result are the swirls you see in an old woodcut. Inside the body, the gouges only cut a hatch.

ANALOGY: the carving is simulated and real wood has no contour field. LIT: the tardigrade outline has 8 legs (4 pairs) and the body is about 0.5 mm long. Drag on the block to cut with your own gouge. Every pull is a reservation on the ledger, and so is every cut you make.
