ASTATINE · Z 85

At, 6p5 outermost, in a 10-shell frame

THE RULE

85 electrons sit in their real ground-state shells (K 2 · L 8 · M 18 · N 32 · O 18 · P 7 · Q 0 · 8 0 · 9 0 · 10 0). Each orbits and jitters as an agent. Heat or a tap lifts one into a higher shell (up to shell 10); it relaxes back and emits a photon.

Tap near an electron to excite it. The same ?seed= always gives the same run.
