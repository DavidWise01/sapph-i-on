Marbled Endpaper

This is a bath of size, with ink floated on top. Every drop of ink pushes all the ink already there outward by an exact area-preserving rule. Every pull of the stylus or comb drags the surface along by a rule that falls off with distance. Nothing else happens. The feathers, the nonpareil and the bouquets of marbled paper all emerge from those two moves applied over and over. The inks are the eight q.d colours. Drag to draw a stylus line. The drops, the combs and your strokes are each a reservation on the ledger.

LIT: ebru (Turkish paper marbling, 15th c. onward). MODEL: the mathematical marbling of Lu, Jaffer et al. (2012). A drop of radius r at c maps p to c + (p−c)·√(1 + r²/|p−c|²), which preserves area. A tine line moves p by z·u^{d}, where d is p's distance from the line. ANALOGY: the q.d inks.
