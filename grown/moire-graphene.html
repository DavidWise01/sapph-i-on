LITStack two graphene sheets and twist one. At about 1.1°, the first "magic" angle, the electronic bands near zero energy go flat. Cao et al. (Nature, 2018) found correlated insulators at half filling and superconductivity up to 1.7 K in this twisted bilayer graphene. The superlattice period is L = a / (2 sin(θ/2)), with graphene's lattice constant a ≈ 0.246 nm.
