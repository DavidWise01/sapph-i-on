Eu:YSO — six hours

LITZhong et al. (Nature, 2015) stored coherence in a ground-state hyperfine transition of ¹⁵¹Eu³⁺ dopants in yttrium orthosilicate (Y₂SiO₅). With dynamic decoupling at 2 K the coherence time reached 370 ± 60 minutes, about six hours. The decoherence rate was 8 × 10⁻⁵ per second. They note spins carried at 9 km/h in such a crystal would lose coherence more slowly per distance than light in optical fibre. The transition is optically addressable, which makes it a candidate quantum memory.

MODELAn aurora of nuclear spins in the void. Each spin precesses at a slightly different rate and the ensemble fans out (dephasing). Press decouple: a π pulse flips every phase, and the fan refocuses into an echo. The clock compresses six hours into a minute.
