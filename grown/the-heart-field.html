THE HEART FIELD

rest, fire, recover — and the spirals come by themselves

THE RULE

A resting cell fires if at least θ of its 8 neighbours are firing. A firing cell then steps through K−2 refractory states and back to rest. The seed sets K and θ, and the field starts from a few broken wave fragments.

Tap to fire a patch. The same ?seed= always gives the same run.
