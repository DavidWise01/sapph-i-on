THE ECHO OF SPINS

lost in phase, found again at 2τ

THE RULE

160 spins precess at slightly different rates, so their summed magnetisation fades. At time τ a π-pulse mirrors every phase, and the fast spins are now behind. At 2τ they all line up again.
