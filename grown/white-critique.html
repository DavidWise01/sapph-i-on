The cipher page is exact arithmetic, not a claimed decryption. Even letters cancel, 13 odd letters survive, the coefficients sum to 26 (the line count), the value is 356, and s carries a coefficient of 3 at the −t+s break. White is computed, not painted: six shells solve to D65, recombining Newton's bands returns the source, a complementary chord crosses D65, and Bradford maps illuminant A back.
