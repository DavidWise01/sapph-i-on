THE FAIR MARKET

everyone starts equal, and the trades are fair

THE RULE

600 agents start with 100 each. Random pairs pool their money and split it at a random point; the seed may let each agent save a share λ. Nobody can go into debt.

Tap to reset everyone to equal. The same ?seed= always gives the same run.
